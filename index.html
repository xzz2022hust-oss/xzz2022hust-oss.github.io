Zizhuo Xu

I am a researcher in marine engineering and autonomous systems. I received my B.Eng. in Marine Engineering from the School of Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

My research combines physical structure and data-driven methods to build reliable models of nonlinear dynamical systems. I focus on system identification, physics-informed machine learning, parameter identifiability, uncertainty quantification, and ship maneuvering prediction.

Research

Differentiable MMG Identification

Built a differentiable 45-parameter ship maneuvering model and reduced calibration time from about five days to 20 minutes. Fisher-guided selection improved held-out berthing RMSE from 1.44 m to 1.19 m using 13 calibrated parameters.

Regime-Gated MMG Experts

Developed identifiability-constrained expert specialization across operating regimes, reducing mean 80-second trajectory RMSE from 0.740 m to 0.486 m.

Hybrid Disturbance Modeling

Combined an MMG physics backbone with disturbance-specific residual experts and hierarchical uncertainty attribution for changing environments and component degradation.

Intelligent Waste-Sorting System

Led mechanical design and prototype integration for a YOLOv8-based waste-sorting system with a robotic arm and sliding platform.
