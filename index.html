About me

I am a prospective M.Sc. student. I received my B.Eng. in Marine Engineering from the School of Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

My undergraduate research focused on ship maneuvering models, including differentiable MMG modeling, parameter identification, and physics–data hybrid prediction under multi-source disturbances.

I am interested in extending this work toward the modeling and control of diverse autonomous systems. In particular, I want to explore machine learning for system modeling, model predictive control, time-series forecasting, and fault diagnosis, together with differentiable simulation and data-driven discovery of governing ODEs and PDEs.

Research interests

Modeling and control of autonomous systems

Physics-based, data-driven, and hybrid methods for marine vehicles and other autonomous dynamical systems.

Data-driven discovery of ODEs and PDEs

Learning interpretable governing equations and dynamics from observational and experimental data.

Machine learning for modeling and control

Machine learning methods for system modeling, identification, and model predictive control (MPC).

Time-series forecasting and fault diagnosis

Learning temporal dynamics, detecting abnormal behavior, and diagnosing faults in complex systems.

Differentiable simulation

End-to-end differentiable physical models for efficient calibration, sensitivity analysis, and control.
