About me

I am a researcher in marine engineering and autonomous systems. I received my B.Eng. in Marine Engineering from the School of Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My undergraduate research was supervised by Prof. Xianzhou Wang.

My work studies how physical structure and data can be combined to build reliable models of nonlinear dynamical systems. I am particularly interested in system identification, state estimation, physics-informed machine learning, parameter identifiability, and uncertainty quantification. Ship maneuvering is my main application domain, with broader interests in autonomous marine vehicles and safety-critical control.

My recent research develops differentiable MMG ship models, identifiability-guided parameter selection, and modular residual learning for operating-condition changes and multi-source disturbances.
